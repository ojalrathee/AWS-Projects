01 — About the work

I travel alone so the landscape can speak first, and I photograph it before I speak at all.

My work begins with distance — a bus that runs once a day, a trailhead with no signage, a village reachable only on foot. Solo travel strips away the noise of shared itineraries and leaves room for the kind of attention a place actually deserves.

I think of each series as a silent documentary: no narration, no staged moments, just light behaving honestly. Between frames, I try to arrive as a guest — sharing meals, learning enough language to be polite, and letting the people I meet shape the story as much as the terrain does.

Eight years and forty-one countries in, the practice hasn't changed much: walk further than feels efficient, wait longer than feels comfortable, and photograph what's left when the hurry wears off.
